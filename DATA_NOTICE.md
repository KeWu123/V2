# PROMISE12 data notice

This workspace contains data from the PROMISE12 (Prostate MR Image Segmentation 2012) challenge.

- Official dataset record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8014041
- Required paper citation: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2013.12.002

The dataset record states that the data may be used for research and that citation of the PROMISE12 paper is mandatory. The dataset remains subject to the terms published by its original authors and distributor.
